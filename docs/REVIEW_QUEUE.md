## 3. FLEX: "Test end" label versus extinction diameter (decision D22)

The outcome class comes from the report's "Test end" label (Extinction = self_extinguished, Completion = burned_to_completion, Disruption = other). The visible flame extinction diameter is only a consistency check: a positive diameter should go with "Extinction", a dash or zero with the other labels. **58 rows disagree** (listed below). The label wins in the data; the diameter is kept in `ext.extinction_diameter_mm`.
