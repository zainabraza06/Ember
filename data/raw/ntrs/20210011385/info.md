## Contents (as read for M2)
NASA/TM-20210011385, 144 PDF pages, October 2021. Chapters 1-5: summary and individual PI reports (Bhattacharjee, Ferkul, Fernandez-Pello, Miller and Wichman, Olson, T'ien). Appendix A (PDF p.111-121) has six test matrices (Tables A.1-A.6, one per PI) with per-test conditions AND a free-text "Test observations and anomalies (ignited, spread, extinguished, burn time, color, etc.)" column. All but 3 of the 129 PSI-25 test IDs appear in Appendix A text (O20, O21, O22 not found by the scan). Table 5.1 (PDF p.65) gives 20 narrow-channel tests (M1-M20) with velocity (cm/s), spread rate (mm/s), burn time (min), initial and final O2. Table 4.2 (PDF p.56) gives velocity and O2 ranges for PMMA rods. Table 7.1 (PDF p.104) summarizes flat SIBAL fabric tests in concurrent flow. Appendix C lists anomalies; Appendix D heat release estimates (Table D.2 burn durations).
Outcomes are narrative, not a coded label. Flow in the Appendix A tables is given as fan/air display codes and potentiometer settings; one test narrative (B21) pairs an "air display" of 5 with "Ignition at 5 cm/s", suggesting air display is in cm/s, but the report text read does not define it. Appendix A also carries columns absent from the PSI-25 table (igniter, flow direction word such as Opposed/Concurrent, sample distance, observations). No per-test transcription was made in M2.
