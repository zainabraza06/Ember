## Contents (as read for M2)
NASA/TP-2015-216046, 314 PDF pages. Table IX (PDF p.26-34) lists results by test: test number, identifier, date/time, fuel, pressure (mmHg), initial composition (mole fractions O2, N2, CO2, He), initial droplet diameter, visible flame extinction diameter, burning rate, burn time, and "Test end" (Extinction / Disruption / Completion), for test numbers 1-280 (276 found by a text scan). Tables I-VIII (PDF p.16-21) give the test matrices. Appendix B has one figure per test (droplet diameter squared and flame diameter vs time, with a caption note per test). The legend (PDF p.25) defines most columns and says "-" in extinction diameter means no extinction because the droplet burned to completion or a disruption ended the test.
Cross-check with the PSI-69 table: PSI lists test numbers 1-274 (274 rows); the report lists 1-280, so tests 275-280 appear only in the report. PDF title reads "March 2009 to December 2011" while Appendix A text says "March 2009 through December 2010". Report confirms the columns "O" and "CO" in the PSI table are O2 and CO2 (Table IX headings) and that pressure is in mmHg. Report states electronic data files are on a DVD addendum (not examined).
